JUN 23 2026

Fête nationale Show in Fatima

L'Anse-aux-Baleiniers, Fatima

A festive evening under the stars with local artists, a bonfire and fireworks — come sing, dance and celebrate Québec's National Holiday with family and friends!

Price : Free

Free for everybody.

View Program

Join us for a festive and heartwarming evening where music, good vibes, and local pride will be celebrated!

On stage, discover a lineup of local artists who will have you singing, dancing, and grooving to the rhythm of our greatest hits from yesterday and today.

Bring your chair, your flag, and your biggest smile… and come experience a moment of pride, togetherness, and fun under the stars!

🚐 RVs and campervans are welcome on site.

JUN 27–28 2026

Sécuri-Fête — Canadian Snowbirds

L'Anse-aux-Baleiniers, Fatima

Two spectacular days with the Canadian Forces Snowbirds: breathtaking aerial demonstrations, public safety exhibitors and workshops for the whole family!

Price : Free

Free for everybody.

View Program

On June 27 and 28, 2026, don’t miss two spectacular days of safety demonstrations and airshows featuring the legendary Canadian Forces Snowbirds! Watch these elite pilots perform breathtaking aerial maneuvers, showcasing extraordinary teamwork, discipline and skill.

A must-see for aviation enthusiasts, families and anyone who admires Canadian excellence!

🚐 RVs and campervans are not permitted on site.

🚌 A free shuttle is available from the Fatima community hall (4, chemin Ernest, Fatima). Departure at 1:00 PM toward the site, return at approximately 5:00 PM.

Saturday, June 27, 2026 — 1:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m.

Several exhibitors working in public safety and emergency response will be present:

  • Sûreté du Québec (Quebec Provincial Police)
  • Fire Department (Municipality of the Magdalen Islands)
  • Magdalen Islands Ambulance Service
  • Rangers
  • Marine Response Service

Event agenda :

  • 1:00 p.m. — Official Ceremony
  • 2:00 p.m. — Snowbirds Air Show
  • 3:00 p.m. — Presentation of the rangers of the Islands as well as the Magdalen Islands members of the Canadian Armed Forces who will be present on site.

Sunday, June 28, 2026 — 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM

Exhibitors return, with themed workshops offered to the public:

  • Drug and alcohol awareness
  • Cybersecurity and online threats
  • Use of fire extinguishers and smoke detectors

Around 2:00 PM, a special demonstration is planned with the arrival of the Canadian Armed Forces’ CH-149 search and rescue helicopter.

Free Admission

AUG 29 2026

Le Grand rassemblement — 6th Edition

L'Anse-aux-Baleiniers, Fatima

Nestled between sea and cliffs, Anse aux Baleiniers hosts a vibrant end-of-summer festival: silent auction, bonfires, fire sculptures and Éric Lapointe headlining!

Price : $55

Free for children aged 12 and under.

View Program

Come celebrate the 6th edition of this end-of-summer festival in one of the most beautiful spots in the Îles-de-la-Madeleine! Nestled between sea and cliffs, Anse aux Baleiniers opens its arms for a vibrant, festive celebration deeply rooted in Madelinot soul.

Whether you’re a longtime islander or passing visitor, this festival is an invitation to fully experience the spirit of the Îles: the sea, the music, the laughter and unforgettable encounters. Don’t miss this final festive stop before autumn arrives!

🚐 RVs and campervans are welcome on site.

Event agenda

  • 1:00 PM — Chinese auction opens (prizes worth $5,000 and more)
  • 7:30 PM — Bonfire with “Sea and Fire Sculpture” performance Sculptors: Jacques Aucoin, Raynald Chevarie, Jeannot Longuépée, Gilbert Chevarie, Laurent Déraspe, Annie Morin
  • 19h35 PM — Breen, Lebœuf / Offenback
  • 10:10 PM — MysterAct - MZ Laser
  • 9h20 — Fireworks
  • 9:30 PM — Éric Lapointe
