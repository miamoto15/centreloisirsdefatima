This page describes each round of the evening, its game format, variant and prize. Seven rounds are part of the mandatory booklet; three additional rounds are optional but recommended to maximize your chances.

Each "face" on a sheet corresponds to a bingo card: a 9-face sheet contains nine distinct cards. The more faces you have, the better your chances of winning.

Split-the-pot tickets are also available from opening time, at $1 each — each ticket increases the pot and you could leave with half the total amount.

{{< tour-card title="Bonanza" color="#7b1fa2" prix="50 %" img="img/bingo/Bonanza.png" alt="Bonanza sheet" special="true" >}}
A very popular optional card — perfect for spicing up the evening! The Bonanza sheet has two faces. The goal: complete an entire card. The prize is half the total amount collected from Bonanza sheet sales during the evening.
{{< /tour-card >}}

{{< tour-card num="1st round" title="Blue — regular" color="#1565c0" prix="$150" img="img/bingo/1er tour - Bleu.png" alt="1st round Blue" >}}
A classic and accessible round: complete a line, a column, the four corners or a diagonal to win. A great way to get into the rhythm and try to take home the $150 prize.
{{< /tour-card >}}

{{< tour-card num="2nd round" title="Orange — regular" color="#e65100" prix="$150" img="img/bingo/2e tour - Orange.png" alt="2nd round Orange" >}}
Same principle as the first round: a regular game where you just need to complete a line, a column, the four corners or a diagonal. Fast, friendly and perfect for sharing the excitement with other players.
{{< /tour-card >}}

{{< tour-card num="3rd round" title="Green — regular" color="#2e7d32" prix="$150" img="img/bingo/3e tour - Vert.png" alt="3rd round Green" >}}
Another regular round in the same format — yet another chance to score a nice win by completing a line, a column, the four corners or a diagonal.
{{< /tour-card >}}

{{< tour-card num="4th round" title="Yellow — special game" color="#c8900a" prix="$200" img="img/bingo/4e tour - Jaune.png" alt="4th round Yellow" >}}
This round introduces an original twist: a "special" number is announced (the last digit of the drawn number) and you must mark on your card all numbers ending with that digit (for example, if 26 is called, mark 6, 16, 26, 36...).

The first to complete the required pattern wins the prize. The pattern changes each week (V, chair, diamond, etc.) and is displayed on the board and screens.
{{< /tour-card >}}

{{< tour-card title="Double Action" color="#00838f" prix="$200" img="img/bingo/Double Action.png" alt="Double Action" special="true" imgwidth="140px" >}}
A fun optional card: each special square has two associated numbers. When one of the two numbers is drawn, both are marked on the card. The objective remains to complete an entire card — a fun and different way to play.
{{< /tour-card >}}

{{< tour-card num="5th round" title="Pink — regular" color="#ad1457" prix="$150" img="img/bingo/5e tour - Rose.png" alt="5th round Pink" >}}
Back to the regular format: complete a line, a column, the four corners or a diagonal to win. A simple round that's always just as exciting.
{{< /tour-card >}}

{{< tour-card num="6th round" title="Grey — regular" color="#546e7a" prix="$150" img="img/bingo/6e tour - Gris.png" alt="6th round Grey" >}}
Another regular round, same rules as before. Each round is a new opportunity — stay focused, surprises can happen at any moment.
{{< /tour-card >}}

{{< tour-card title="Cumulative Prize" color="#b45309" prix="$300 and up" img="img/bingo/Tour cumulatif.jpg" alt="Cumulative round" special="true" >}}
A highly anticipated progressive variant: the prize starts at $300 with a limited number of balls (e.g. 15 balls). The pattern changes each week (V, chair, diamond, etc.) and is displayed on the board and screens.

If no one completes the pattern within the set number of balls, a consolation prize of $300 is awarded to the next winner and the pot increases by $100 the following week (one extra ball and a higher amount). The jackpot can rise up to a maximum of $1,300, at which point the prize must be won with no ball limit.
{{< /tour-card >}}

{{< tour-card num="7th round" title="Olive — regular + jackpot" color="#558b2f" img="img/bingo/7e tour - Olive.png" alt="7th round Olive" >}}
The final round is played on the same sheet and offers three ways to win:

- **Regular at $100:** complete a line, a column, the four corners or a diagonal.
- **Bonus game at $150:** a special pattern, different each week; complete it to win $150.
- **Full card — jackpot at $1,000:** complete the entire card to try for the grand prize.
{{< /tour-card >}}
