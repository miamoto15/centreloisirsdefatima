Children and adults alike, get ready for a magical day filled with play, laughter, and creativity! The Children's Festival, for its **first edition**, offers activities and surprises to discover! Throughout the day, enjoy continuous entertainment, interactive and creative games, and challenges of skill and dexterity.

Also, be sure to meet our mascot, always ready to make children laugh and dance! Come and experience a day of family fun, with your feet in the sand and your head full of unforgettable memories. Bring your smile, your energy, and your sunscreen!

**🚐 RVs and campervans are not permitted on site.**
