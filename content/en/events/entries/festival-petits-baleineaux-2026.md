Children and adults alike, get ready for two magical days filled with play, laughter, and creativity! The Children's Festival, for its **first edition**, offers activities and surprises to discover! Throughout the festival, enjoy continuous entertainment, interactive and creative games, and challenges of skill and dexterity.

Also, be sure to meet our mascot, always ready to make children laugh and dance! Come and experience two days of family fun, with your feet in the sand and your head full of unforgettable memories. Bring your smile, your energy, and your sunscreen!

## Saturday, July 11 — 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM

- **11:00 AM** — [Atchoum](https://www.ppscanada.com/artistes/atchoum/)
- **1:00 PM** — [« Rufus le Vélocirator »](https://katomix.com/fr/nos-dinosaures#)
- **1:30 PM to 5:00 PM** — [Mini-Paleontologists](https://katomix.com/fr/za-mini-paleontologues): full dinosaur-themed zone with interactive archaeological dig stations
- Roaming animation with Hulky the Triceratops and baby dinosaurs
- Airbrush temporary tattoos, foam cannon and inflatable games
