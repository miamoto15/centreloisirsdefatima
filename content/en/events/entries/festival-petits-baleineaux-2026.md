Big and small, get ready for two magical days of play, laughter and creativity! The Children's Festival, in its **very first edition**, offers activities and surprises at every turn — continuous entertainment, interactive games and hands-on fun throughout both days.

Come live two days of family happiness with unforgettable memories. Bring your smile, your energy and your sunscreen!

## Saturday, July 11 — 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM

- **11:00 AM** — [Atchoum](https://www.ppscanada.com/artistes/atchoum/) show
- **1:00 PM** — "Rufus the Velociraptor" show
- **1:30 PM to 5:00 PM** — Mini-Paleontologists: full dinosaur-themed zone with interactive archaeological dig stations
- Roaming animation with Hulky the Triceratops and baby dinosaurs
- Airbrush temporary tattoos, foam cannon and inflatable games

## Sunday, July 12 — 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM

- **1:00 PM** — "Rufus the Velociraptor" show
- **1:30 PM to 5:00 PM** — Mini-Paleontologists (full dinosaur zone) with interactive dig stations
- Roaming animation with Hulky the Triceratops and baby dinosaurs
- Airbrush temporary tattoos, foam cannon and inflatable games
