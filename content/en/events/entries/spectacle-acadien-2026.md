Come celebrate the richness and warmth of Acadian culture at our show in Fatima! This festive event honours music, dance, gastronomy and the traditions of the Acadian people, whose history is intimately tied to that of the Îles-de-la-Madeleine.

Whether you're Acadian or simply curious, come experience a colourful evening filled with infectious rhythms, regional flavours and warm encounters!
