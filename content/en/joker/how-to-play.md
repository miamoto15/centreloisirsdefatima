## Want to try your luck?

Buy a ticket for just **$5**, try to find the Joker, and win the grand prize — it grows every week until someone finds it! Every Tuesday, one ticket is drawn at random: the holder comes to pick a card from **51 cards… and a Joker**. Find it and the jackpot is yours. If not, the card is removed from the deck and your odds improve the following week!
{{% /hero %}}

{{< spacer 20 >}}

{{< step-card num="1" icon="🎟️" title="Buy your ticket" >}}
Visit one of our official sales points and get a ticket for only **$5**. Each ticket gives you a chance to participate in the weekly draw.
{{< /step-card >}}

{{< step-card num="2" icon="🎯" title="Take part in the Tuesday draw" >}}
Every **Tuesday at 4:00 PM** at Coop l'Éveil, a winning ticket is drawn at random from all tickets sold during the week. The ticket holder wins **30% of the weekly jackpot** (minus administration fees)!
{{< /step-card >}}

{{< step-card num="3" icon="⏰" title="Respond quickly" warning="true" >}}
If your ticket is drawn, you will be contacted. You have **less than 24 hours** to respond and confirm your participation, otherwise a new draw may be held.
{{< /step-card >}}

{{< step-card num="4" icon="🃏" title="Try to find the Joker" >}}
The next day (**Wednesday**), the winner is invited to draw a card from a deck of **51 cards… and one Joker**! If you find the Joker, you win the grand prize. If not, the drawn card is removed from the deck, increasing your chances the following week.
{{< /step-card >}}

{{< step-card num="5" icon="📈" title="A jackpot that grows every week" >}}
As long as the Joker is not found, the grand prize keeps growing. Each card removed reduces the remaining possibilities — the more you play regularly, **the better your chances**!
{{< /step-card >}}
