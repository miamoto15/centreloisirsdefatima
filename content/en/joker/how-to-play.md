## Want to try your luck?

1. **Buy your ticket**  
Visit one of our official sales points and get a ticket for only $5. Each ticket gives you a chance to participate in the weekly draw.

2. **Take part in the Tuesday draw**  
Every Tuesday at 4:00 PM at Coop l’Éveil, a winning ticket is drawn at random from all tickets sold during the week. The ticket holder wins 30% of the weekly jackpot (minus administration fees)!

3. **Respond quickly**  
If your ticket is drawn, you will be contacted. You have less than 24 hours to respond and confirm your participation, otherwise a new draw may be held.

4. **Try to find the Joker**  
The next day, Wednesday, the winner is invited to draw a card from a deck of 51 cards... and one Joker! If you find the Joker, you win the grand prize. If not, the drawn card is removed from the deck, increasing your chances the following week!

5. **A jackpot that grows every week**  
As long as the Joker is not found, the grand prize keeps growing. The more you play, the better your chances!

**Prize distribution:**
Distribution of weekly sales revenue (minus 12% administration fees):

* Winning ticket: 30%
* Joker grand prize: 25%
* Fatima Recreation Committee: 45%
