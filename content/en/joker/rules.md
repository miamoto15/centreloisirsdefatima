## "Find the Joker" Draw

**Holder**: Fatima Community Center, M.I., Inc.  
**License**: L-02004  
**Lottery**: "FIND THE JOKER"

The "FIND THE JOKER" lottery offers cumulative prizes, meaning the value of the "jackpot" increases as long as the "JOKER" is not drawn. The goal is to draw the "JOKER". Each ticket is sold for $5.

**Distribution of weekly sales revenue (minus 12% administration fees):**

* Winning ticket 30%
* Joker jackpot 25%
* Fatima Community Center Committee 45%

With this lottery, you have two chances to win each week. Ticket sales end at 11:00 PM on Monday, and the respective prize amounts will be known before the draw. A first draw from all tickets sold during the week will take place every Tuesday at 4:00 PM at "L'Éveil" (Coop de Fatima).

The holder of the drawn ticket will automatically win 30%. If the ticket holder is not present, they will be contacted by phone (the number written on their ticket) and must respond before Wednesday 9:00 AM, otherwise a second draw will be held on Wednesday morning at the same location.

The holder of the drawn ticket will be invited to draw a card for the "jackpot", this time at the "Place des Iles" shopping center (Lavernière), Wednesday at 1:00 PM. If the ticket holder cannot be physically present for the second draw (drawing a card), they may designate another person by proxy.

During this second draw, the winner (or the designated person) must draw a card from a regular deck of 52 cards plus ONE JOKER, the goal being to draw THE JOKER, i.e., the jackpot which represents another 25%, plus the accumulated prize (if any). If THE JOKER is not drawn, the drawn card will be recorded and removed from the deck, and the jackpot will continue to grow until someone draws THE JOKER. After each draw, the remaining cards will be revealed to the public, confirming that the JOKER was present in the deck during the draw.

Each week after the Wednesday 1:00 PM draw, all non-winning tickets will be destroyed. Once the Wednesday draw is over, ticket sales for the next week may begin.

To participate in the draw, you must be 18 years or older and a resident of Quebec. The information on the ticket must be complete and legible; it is the responsibility of the ticket holder.

The rules of this lottery are posted at the two locations where the draws take place: Coop IGA l'Éveil in Fatima and Place des Iles shopping center in Lavernière.

For any other information, you can contact us by email: [info@centreloisirsfatima.com](mailto:info@centreloisirsfatima.com)
