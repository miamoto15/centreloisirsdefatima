## Buy your tickets

You can buy your Joker tickets for the Fatima Community Center at several points of sale. Here are the available options:

---

### 1. Interac Payment

{{< grid cols=2 width="60%,40%" >}}

  {{% cell valign="middle" %}}

**Interac sales point:**

- Payment by Interac transfer to Auxiles Décoste
- Send a text message to 581-887-7495
- Please indicate in the message:
  - How many tickets you want
  - Your name to put on the tickets
  - Your home address if you wish
  - The phone number to reach you
- A photo of your tickets will be sent to you afterwards.

***Please note that only residents of Quebec can participate in the lottery.***
