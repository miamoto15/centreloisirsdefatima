## 🎟️ Buy your tickets

There are several ways to purchase your Joker tickets: by Interac transfer, directly from one of our official sellers, or at a participating store in the Magdalen Islands. Tickets are sold for **$5 each**. Choose the method that works best for you!

---

### 💸 Interac Payment

{{< interac-contact img="img/Interact.jpg" alt="Interac Logo" name="Auxiles Décoste" phone="581-887-7495" >}}
Send a text message indicating:

- How many tickets you want
- Your full name to put on the tickets
- Your home address (required for validation)
- A phone number to reach you if needed

You will receive a confirmation along with a photo of your tickets. Payment is processed at the time of the order.
{{< /interac-contact >}}

{{% callout type="warning" %}}
Only **residents of Quebec** can participate in the lottery.
{{% /callout %}}
